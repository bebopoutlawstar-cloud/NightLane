Child Safety Standards

Last updated October 4, 2026

The short version

  • NightLane has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Anyone who shares, asks for, or encourages this kind of content is banned, and we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement.
  • You can report a message right inside the app, or email support@nightlane.chat.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 (or your local emergency number) first.

1. About NightLane

NightLane is a group-chat app at nightlane.chat and on Google Play, made and run by Cole Eggly in Las Vegas, Nevada, USA. You must be 13 or older to use it.

2. What is never allowed

The following are strictly prohibited on NightLane, in messages, photos, videos, voice messages, stickers, profile pictures, usernames, room names, and anywhere else in the app:

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM): images, videos, drawings, AI-generated content, or text that sexualizes a minor.
  • Grooming: building trust with a minor in order to sexually exploit or abuse them.
  • Asking a minor for sexual images or sexual conversation, or sending sexual content to a minor.
  • Sextortion: threatening to share someone's private images, or demanding images, money, or anything else.
  • Sharing, trading, or linking to CSAM anywhere, or helping others find it.
  • Trafficking, or any content that promotes or encourages the sexual exploitation of children.

These rules are part of our Terms of Service. There are no warnings or second chances for breaking them.

3. How NightLane is built to keep teens safe

  • 13+ only. Everyone confirms they are 13 or older when they sign up. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete it.
  • No strangers by default. Rooms can only be joined with an invite link or code that a member shares. Direct messages only work between people who are friends or already share a room. There is no public directory of rooms.
  • Block anyone, any time. Blocked people can't message you.
  • Report any message. Every message has a Report option that goes straight to NightLane's review queue.
  • Edits can't hide abuse. When a message is edited, a private copy of the earlier version is kept so reports can be reviewed fairly.
  • No location sharing. NightLane doesn't collect or share your location, and photo location data (GPS) is removed before upload.

4. How to report a concern

In the app

  • Press and hold the message, tap Report 🚩, pick a reason (use Inappropriate content for child safety concerns), and add any details.
  • You can also block the person right away from the same menu.

By email

Email support@nightlane.chat with the subject "Child safety report". Include the username, the room or chat, and what happened. You don't need to have a NightLane account to report.

Outside NightLane

  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.
  • In the US, you can report child sexual exploitation directly to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.
  • Outside the US, contact your local police or your country's child protection hotline.

5. What we do when something is reported

  • Child safety reports are reviewed first, as quickly as possible. We aim to act within 24 hours.
  • Content that breaks these standards is removed, and the account responsible is banned.
  • Apparent CSAM is reported to the NCMEC CyberTipline, as US law requires, and to law enforcement when appropriate.
  • We preserve the reported content and related account information for law enforcement as the law requires, even after it's removed from the app.
  • We cooperate with valid requests from law enforcement in child safety investigations.

6. Laws we follow

NightLane complies with child safety laws that apply to it, including US federal law on reporting child sexual abuse material (18 U.S.C. § 2258A), and works with the relevant regional and national authorities.
