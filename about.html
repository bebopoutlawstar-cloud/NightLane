What is NightLane?

A private, retro neon punk messenger

NightLane is a private, retro neon punk messenger inspired by gamer culture, built for small groups who want a place to really be themselves.

Features

  • Private rooms and DMs for your crew
  • Voice messages, stickers, and polls
  • Game & anime backlog tracking
  • Daily habit tracker that awards stats
  • Built-in mini-games
  • Installable as an app (PWA) on phone and desktop

Who it's for

NightLane is not a public social network. It's invite-style chat for people who already know each other: no ads, no trackers, no algorithmic feed.

You must be 13 or older to use NightLane.

Who runs it

NightLane is operated by Cole Eggly, Las Vegas, Nevada, USA. Questions? See our Contact & Support page.
