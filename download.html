Quick answers

Google Play says NightLane isn't available?

You haven't joined the tester group yet, or you joined with a different Google account than the one on your phone. Join it with your phone's account, wait a minute, then open the Play link again.

Is it free?

Yes. NightLane is free to use.

Is the app different from the website?

No, it's the same NightLane. Your chats, friends and Lanes come with you.

No notifications on iPhone?

iPhones only send them once NightLane is on your Home Screen. Add it, open it from there, and tap Allow.

Who can use NightLane?

Anyone 13 or older.
