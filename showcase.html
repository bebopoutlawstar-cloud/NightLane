Everything a crew needs

Rooms for the group, DMs for the two of you, and a pile of extras that make it feel like your own hangout instead of a utility.

Chat

  • Group rooms and private DMs
  • Replies, pins, edits, polls
  • Reactions, with who reacted
  • Disappearing messages
  • Built-in translation and search

Share

  • Voice messages
  • Photos, videos, GIFs, stickers
  • Spoiler-blurred photos
  • E-cards for birthdays and more

Presence

  • Read receipts and typing dots
  • Online, away and Do Not Disturb
  • A custom status line
  • Birthday cake on your picture

Social Bonds

  • Friendship ranks that grow as you talk
  • Unlock DM backgrounds and bubble styles
  • Checked by the server, so nobody skips ahead

Play

  • Crazy Eights, BS, tic-tac-toe
  • Roll a d20 right in chat
  • Dozens of secret words that set off screen effects

Track

  • Game, anime, movie and TV backlog
  • Your own posters, shared with your crew
  • Daily habit tracker that earns stats

Two sides, two looks

NightLane doesn't just stretch the phone app onto a big screen. Each side has its own personality.

On your phone

A big tilted date stamp on the front screen, angled Persona-style bubbles, "!" and "?" pop-outs, and holiday stickers that change through the year.

> on your PC_

A green terminal layout with keyboard shortcuts and pixel-art holiday sprites under the date.

Made to be yours

Style

  • 17 profile frames
  • Themes and bubble colors
  • Status dot shapes

Safety

  • Block and report
  • Reset your friend link anytime
  • Chats hidden from anyone outside them

Reliable

  • Installs like an app on phone and PC

Build log

Built and shipped in small, tested updates, each with its own version number and changelog note.

Sept 22

First tracked version: rooms, DMs and the neon look.

Late Sept

Renamed to NightLane and moved to nightlane.chat. Games, backlog, Social Bonds and secret effects arrive.

Oct 4

Google Play closed test approved and published. Statuses, e-cards and see-who-reacted ship.

Oct 5

Away status, PC holiday pixel art, weak Wi-Fi fixes, and the About and Contact pages. Version 113.

Next

Voice and video calls in DMs, then the public Google Play launch.

Under the hood

An installable web app (PWA) backed by Supabase, wrapped for Android as a Trusted Web Activity.
